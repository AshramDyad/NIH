# 🌿 Wellness Centre Franchise Project

## 1. Business Concept

Our franchise will offer **holistic wellness services** including:

* Fitness training
* Yoga & meditation
* Nutrition counseling
* Mental health support
* Preventive health check-ups

The focus is on **preventive healthcare and lifestyle management**, not just gym services.

---

## 2. Franchise Model

### Option A: Single-Unit Franchise

One location operated by a franchisee.

### Option B: Multi-Unit Franchise

Franchisee owns multiple branches in a region.

### Option C: Master Franchise

One franchisee controls rights in a city or country.

---

## 3. Revenue Model

* Monthly memberships
* Personal training packages
* Nutrition consultation fees
* Corporate wellness contracts
* Wellness workshops
* Product sales (supplements, fitness gear)

---

## 4. Franchisee Support

We will provide:

* Brand name & trademark usage
* Standard operating procedures (SOPs)
* Staff training programs
* Marketing support
* Business management system
* Ongoing operational guidance

---

## 5. Brand Positioning

Our franchise will be positioned as:

✔ Premium but affordable
✔ Holistic (mind + body + nutrition)
✔ Community-focused
✔ Professional and certified

---

## 6. Target Market

* Urban professionals
* Students
* Families
* Corporate employees
* Health-conscious individuals

---

## 7. Competitive Advantage

* Combination of fitness + mental wellness
* Personalized health programs
* Community engagement activities
* Technology-based booking & tracking system
