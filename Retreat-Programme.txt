# 🌿 National Institute of Holistic Health

## Holistic Wellness Retreat Programme

---

## 1. Programme Title

**“Rebalance & Renew – 5-Day Holistic Wellness Retreat”**

---

## 2. Purpose of the Retreat

The retreat is designed to:

* Restore physical, mental, and emotional balance
* Reduce stress and burnout
* Promote mindful living
* Encourage healthy lifestyle transformation
* Provide immersive holistic healing experience

---

## 3. Target Participants

* Working professionals
* Entrepreneurs & executives
* Students experiencing stress
* Healthcare workers
* Individuals seeking lifestyle reset

---

## 4. Duration Options

* 2-Day Weekend Retreat
* 5-Day Standard Retreat
* 7-Day Advanced Transformation Retreat

---

# 📅 Sample 5-Day Retreat Schedule

## 🌅 Daily Routine Overview

**6:00 AM** – Sunrise Yoga & Breathwork
**7:30 AM** – Detox Breakfast
**9:00 AM** – Wellness Workshop
**11:00 AM** – Nature Therapy / Outdoor Activity
**1:00 PM** – Nutritious Lunch
**3:00 PM** – Personal Reflection / Counseling
**5:00 PM** – Meditation & Sound Healing
**7:00 PM** – Light Dinner
**8:30 PM** – Mindfulness Circle / Journaling

## 5. Core Programme Components

### 🧘 Physical Wellness

* Hatha / Vinyasa Yoga

* Light fitness sessions

### 🧠 Mental & Emotional Wellness

* Stress management workshops
* Mindfulness training
* Emotional resilience sessions

### 🥗 Nutritional Wellness

* Detox meal plans
* Nutrition education
* Healthy cooking demo
* Personalized diet guidance

### 🌿 Holistic Therapies

* Meditation
* Sound healing
* Aromatherapy
* Nature walks
* Digital detox sessions
