# 🌿 Our Wellness Centre Internship Programme

## 1. Programme Title

**“Holistic Wellness Internship Programme”**

---

## 2. Purpose of the Internship

The internship aims to:

* Provide practical industry experience
* Develop professional wellness skills
* Prepare students for careers in health & fitness
* Support the centre’s daily operations

---

## 3. Duration Options

* 4 Weeks (Short-Term Internship)
* 8–12 Weeks (Standard Internship)
* 6 Months (Advanced / Professional Internship)

---

## 4. Eligibility Criteria

* Students in:

  * Health Sciences
  * Nutrition & Dietetics
  * Physical Education
  * Psychology
  * Physiotherapy
  * Business/Marketing (for management interns)

* Good communication skills

* Interest in wellness industry

---

## 5. Internship Departments

### 🏋️ Fitness & Training Department

* Assist trainers
* Conduct basic fitness assessments
* Help design beginner workout plans
* Monitor gym floor safety

### 🥗 Nutrition Department

* Assist with diet planning
* Support client consultations
* Create meal plan templates
* Research nutrition topics

### 🧠 Mental Wellness Department

* Assist in stress management workshops
* Observe counseling sessions (where permitted)
* Help prepare wellness materials

### 📢 Marketing & Administration

* Social media management
* Event promotion
* Membership registration support
* Customer service assistance

---

## 6. Training Provided to Interns

* Orientation & brand training
* Basic wellness knowledge
* Communication skills
* Professional ethics
* Safety procedures

---

## 7. Learning Outcomes

By the end of the internship, interns will:

* Understand wellness centre operations
* Gain hands-on experience
* Improve professional confidence
* Build teamwork skills
* Develop industry exposure

---

## 8. Evaluation System

* Weekly supervisor feedback
* Performance assessment form
* Final evaluation report
* Internship completion certificate

---

## 9. Benefits to Interns

* Practical experience
* Industry networking
* Recommendation letter (based on performance)
* Certificate of completion
