# 🌿 Our Wellness Centre Volunteership Programme

## 1. Programme Title

**“Community Wellness Volunteership Programme”**

---

## 2. Purpose of the Programme

The volunteership programme aims to:

* Promote community health awareness
* Support wellness events and campaigns
* Encourage social responsibility
* Provide leadership opportunities
* Expand outreach to underserved communities

---

## 3. Target Volunteers

* College & university students
* Health and fitness enthusiasts
* Community members
* Retired healthcare professionals
* Corporate social responsibility (CSR) groups

---

## 4. Areas of Volunteering

### 🏥 Community Health Campaigns

* Assist in health screening camps
* Help organize wellness drives
* Distribute awareness materials

### 🧘 Wellness Events

* Support yoga sessions
* Event coordination
* Participant registration

### 📢 Awareness & Outreach

* Social media promotion
* Community presentations
* School wellness education sessions

### 🥗 Nutrition & Lifestyle Support

* Assist in workshops
* Healthy cooking demonstrations
* Lifestyle improvement campaigns

---

## 5. Duration Options

* Event-Based (1–3 days)
* Short-Term (1 month)
* Long-Term (3–6 months)

---

## 6. Training Provided

Before volunteering, participants receive:

* Orientation session
* Basic health & safety training
* Communication guidelines
* Code of conduct briefing

---

## 7. Responsibilities of Volunteers

* Maintain professionalism
* Respect client confidentiality
* Follow safety protocols
* Represent the brand positively

---

## 8. Benefits for Volunteers

* Certificate of appreciation
* Community service hours record
* Recommendation letter (based on performance)
* Networking opportunities
* Leadership development

---

## 9. Programme Impact

* Increased community health awareness
* Stronger brand reputation
* Greater social responsibility
* Positive community engagement
